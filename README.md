# Little Chef

A pretend-play kitchen for kids ages 3 to 6. Pick food from the fridge, chop it, mix it, cook it on the stove, bake it, blend it, decorate it, and feed it to Biscuit the puppy. Colors, counting and letters come up naturally as she plays. An optional picture recipe book (14 recipes) gives gentle hints about which station is next.

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Works offline and installs as an app (PWA) on Android, iPhone/iPad, Windows, Mac and Linux.
- Grown-ups menu: press and hold the gear on the home screen. Set the age level (3–4 or 5–6), voice, voice pitch, and sounds.
- Bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` (and in `js/app.js`) on every change so installed copies update.
